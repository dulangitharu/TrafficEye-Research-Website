Individual contribution

  • Detection modelTrained YOLOv8n on seven classes and compared it with YOLOv5n under matched settings.
  • Clearance modelBuilt the class- and position-aware queue-clearance calculation with the FIFO rule.
  • Calibration studyCalibrated discharge speeds and the 3.12 s safety margin from 11 local clips.
  • Officer applicationBuilt the FastAPI and React application: upload, results, emergency advisory and history.
  • Research toolsCalibration pipeline, lane-marking tool and model comparison server.
  • VerificationBackend acceptance audit and a prototype clearance check.

TrafficEye is one component of the group project Smart Emergency Traffic Light Control System (R26-IT-056). This website presents only this individual component.
